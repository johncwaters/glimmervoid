# Challenge

1. Invoke the `challenging-plans` skill with the output of `render.ts <spec.json> goal` as goal, the output of `render.ts <spec.json> plan` as plan text, and `repoRoot` as repo path. The plan lists the units ready for work; deferred units wait for their open question.
2. Judge the verdicts as `challenging-plans` prescribes. A merge the validator would reject as over the reading budget is rebutted by that failure. A verdict that undoes a `decided` decision applies once its evidence holds in the repo: set that decision's `answer` to what the verdict leaves, its source to `assumed`, its `evidence` to the file:line that check found, and its `why` to `was: <displaced answer>; overturned: <the challenger's because>`, preceded by the overturns a `reaffirmed:` `why` already records. One whose evidence is `unverified` or fails that check is rebutted by the decision; one repeating an overturn in a `reaffirmed:` `why` is rebutted by that record. Keep an overturned decision left without a unit as `isConstraint: true`.
3. Apply the surviving verdicts to the unit nodes and their edges; each unit title takes its revised step line, within 120 characters, its bounds left to the `Bounds:` block. A verdict that changes a design node's code applies on the evidence terms of step 2; keep its displaced code and the challenger's because for the hand off.
4. Set `challengeStamp` to the stamp line, `challengedUnitIds` to the ids of the units ready for work, and status to `challenged`. When every lane dropped out, keep status `sliced` and report the design spec as unstamped. Open the hand off with each overturned decision and its displaced answer, each changed design node with its displaced code and why, and the plan's `Bounds:` block.
5. A design node the operator restores takes back its displaced code. A decision the operator restores after an overturn returns to `decided` with its `why` as `reaffirmed: <the overturn's why>`. Bring back the units and edges the overturn cut or shrank, clear the `isConstraint` it set, set status `sliced` with `challengeStamp` and `challengedUnitIds` removed, and rerun from step 1.
